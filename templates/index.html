Discover INTELLI-GEN'Z

INTELLI-GEN'Z 2K26 is a premier technology-focused symposium organized by Mahendra College of Engineering. It serves as a dynamic platform bringing together visionary students to explore the frontiers of problem-solving, creativity, and artificial intelligence.

Through a carefully curated series of technical and non-technical events, participants will challenge their competitive skills, engage with emerging technologies, and network with like-minded innovators.
